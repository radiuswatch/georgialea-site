Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information ClipNow collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: your videos stay on your device, and we only collect what we need to run your membership.

Your full videos stay on your device. ClipNow analyzes, crops and exports clips inside your web browser. Only the short clips you make leave your device: when you're signed in, so they show up on your other devices, and when you auto-post one.

Information we collect

  • Account and billing details. When you start a plan, Stripe collects your name, email, billing address and payment method. We receive your name, email, plan, billing status and payment history from Stripe. We never receive your full card number.
  • Signing in. When you sign in, we email you a one-time 6-digit sign-in code and link and set a cookie on your device that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. We keep a record of your email and active sign-ins on our server, and we check your plan with Stripe. Signing out removes the cookie and the sign-in record.
  • Auto-posting. If you connect social accounts and use Post now or Schedule, we send the clip you chose, its captions and the accounts you picked to our posting partner, Upload-Post, which posts it for you. Your social account sign-ins happen on the platforms' own pages; ClipNow never sees your passwords. We keep a list of your recent posts and their results. Disconnect accounts any time from the Auto-post panel.
  • Campaigns (Platinum and Corporate). If you create a campaign, we store its details and cover image and show them to Platinum and Corporate members. If you join one, we store the name and payout details you give (such as a PayPal email or Cash App tag), the links and view counts of posts you submit, and the screenshots you upload. The campaign's creator can see these; other clippers can't. Screenshots are stored privately on our server, not on the public website.
  • Affiliate program. If you join, we store your name, email, referral code, the payout details you give, the number of visits to your link, and the referrals and commissions credited to you. To work out commissions, we check with Stripe which customers started a plan through your link and what they paid. Your dashboard shows each referred customer's email only in masked form (the first two letters and the domain).
  • Referral links. If you arrive through an affiliate's link, we save that affiliate's code in your browser for 60 days and add it to your checkout with Stripe, so the right affiliate gets credit. We count the visit, using a one-way hash of your IP address so repeat visits aren't counted twice.
  • Clip sync. When you're signed in with an active plan, the clips in your Studio (not your full videos) are stored in our cloud storage, with their names, titles and posting status, so they appear on your phone and computer. They're private to your account and deleted automatically after 7 days (Silver), 14 days (Gold) or 30 days (Platinum and Corporate), or as soon as you remove them.
  • AI titles. If you tap Write titles with AI, three small still pictures from each clip (and the topic you typed) are sent to Anthropic, which writes the titles. They're used for that one request and not stored by ClipNow.
  • Ask ClipNow chat. If you use the chat on our home page, your questions and the conversation so far are sent to Anthropic to write the answers. We keep a log of the questions and answers, without your IP address or name, to improve our help pages, and we keep only the most recent ones (up to a few thousand). Don't type personal or payment details into the chat.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep the message and your contact details so we can reply.
  • Settings saved in your browser. ClipNow saves things like your trial start date, plan, chosen platforms and recent searches in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them. This stays on your device; we don't receive it. You can clear it anytime in your browser settings.
  • Basic server logs. Like most websites, our host records standard technical details (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent your personal information.

How we use it

  • To provide your plan, process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To send you account emails, such as receipts, trial reminders and notices when a payment fails, with a secure link to update your card.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep ClipNow secure and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process payments and manage subscriptions (Stripe's privacy policy).
  • Upload-Post, our posting partner, when you auto-post a clip (only that clip, its captions and the accounts you chose).
  • Our web host, which stores our website, server logs and email.
  • Cloudflare, whose R2 storage holds your synced clips (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • Resend, which delivers our emails, such as sign-in codes (only your email address and the message).
  • Anthropic, the AI provider behind AI titles and the Ask ClipNow chat (only the pictures, topic or questions described above). Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models (Anthropic's privacy policy).
  • Affiliates, who see the date, plan and a masked email (first two letters and domain) of customers they referred.
  • Campaign creators, who see the name, payout details, posts, view counts and screenshots of clippers who join their campaigns.
  • Video search. If you use the search on our home page, your search terms are sent to the service you search (such as YouTube) to show results.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and ClipNow.

When you follow a link from ClipNow to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, X or another platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep account and billing records while you're a member and for as long as needed afterward for legal, tax and accounting purposes (typically up to 7 years). Records of the payment notices we send are kept for up to 2 years. Campaigns, their posts and screenshots are kept until the campaign is deleted; ask us to delete your campaign data any time. Affiliate records are kept while your affiliate account is open and afterward as long as needed for tax and accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your billing details or email, or cancel, from the billing portal.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We'll respond within 30 days. Some records must be kept for legal reasons.
  • Clear ClipNow's saved settings by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights. Contact us to use them.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, payments are handled by Stripe, and our admin tools are password-protected. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

ClipNow isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll post changes here with a new effective date and, if they're significant, let members know by email.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Send us a message. We usually reply within two business days.
