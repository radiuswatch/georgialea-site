    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    I've never clipped before. Can I do this?

    Yes. That's who this is built for. Upload, press one button, pick the clips you like, post. Follow the daily plan and you'll learn what works by watching your own numbers.

    How do clippers actually make money?

    Mostly three ways: paid clipping campaigns, where a creator or brand pays you per 1,000 views on clips of their content; platform programs that pay for views, which have follower and region requirements; and affiliate or referral links in your bio. ClipNow doesn't pay you. It makes the posting fast enough that you can do it daily.

    Will I make money every day?

    Nobody can honestly promise that. Earnings depend on views, and views take time to build. What we can promise is that you'll spend minutes, not hours, on each batch of clips, so you can keep showing up long enough to find what works.

    Can I clip anyone's videos?

    No. Clip your own content, or content from creators who give permission, which every paid clipping campaign does. Posting someone else's video without permission can get your account struck and your payouts refused.

    Does my video get uploaded to your servers?

    No. Clipping runs entirely in your browser, so your video stays on your device. When one-click posting arrives, clips will be uploaded only to post them to the accounts you connect, and deleted after 30 days.

    How do I cancel?

    From your account page, any time, in two clicks. No calls, no emails, no "are you sure" mazes.
