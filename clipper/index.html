Campaigns · Platinum & Corporate

Paid clipping campaigns

Streamers and podcasters post a campaign and pay clippers for the views their clips get. Clippers join, post, and report their views. Creators approve the views and pay clippers directly. ClipNow keeps track of it all and never takes a cut.

▶ Watch: how to create a campaign 60 sec
How to create a campaign for streamers & podcasters
  1. Sign in with your Platinum account and open Campaigns.
  2. Tap Create a campaign.
  3. Name it and add your creator name (your channel or show), so clippers know whose content it is.
  4. Say what clippers should make (which videos, what kind of moments) and list your rules, like clip length or crediting you in the caption.
  5. Add a link to your videos: a Google Drive or Dropbox folder, or your YouTube channel. Only clippers who join can see it.
  6. Set what you pay per 1,000 views. Add a total budget if you want a limit; ClipNow won't let you approve views past it. Add an end date if the campaign should stop.
  7. Pick the platforms clippers can post on, add any required tags (like @you or #ad), and say how and when you pay (for example "PayPal, every Friday").
  8. Tap Publish campaign. It's live right away for Platinum clippers. Share clipnow.vip/campaigns with clippers you know.
  9. When clippers post, open your campaign. Under Waiting for review, open each post and its screenshot, then tap Approve views (or Don't approve).
  10. Pay each clipper what they're owed using their payout details, then tap Record payment so you both see it's paid.

You can edit your campaign, or close it to new posts, any time. ClipNow doesn't hold or send money; you pay clippers directly.

How to join and get paid for clippers
  1. Open a campaign under Browse and read its rate, rules and payment terms.
  2. Tap Join campaign with your name and where to pay you (PayPal, Cash App…). Only the creator sees it.
  3. Tap Get the videos, then Clip it in the Studio. The campaign's required tags are added for you.
  4. Post your clip, then come back and Submit a post: its link, its views right now, and a screenshot showing the views.
  5. As views grow, tap Update views with a new screenshot. The creator approves the views and pays you directly.

    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    I've never clipped before. Can I do this?

    Yes. That's who this is built for. Upload, press one button, pick the clips you like, post. Follow the daily plan and you'll learn what works by watching your own numbers.

    How do clippers actually make money?

    Mostly three ways: paid clipping campaigns, where a creator or brand pays you per 1,000 views on clips of their content; platform programs that pay for views, which have follower and region requirements; and affiliate or referral links in your bio. ClipNow doesn't pay you. It makes the posting fast enough that you can do it daily.

    Will I make money every day?

    Nobody can honestly promise that. Earnings depend on views, and views take time to build. What we can promise is that you'll spend minutes, not hours, on each batch of clips, so you can keep showing up long enough to find what works.

    Can I clip anyone's videos?

    No. Clip your own content, or content from creators who give permission, which every paid clipping campaign does. Posting someone else's video without permission can get your account struck and your payouts refused.

    Does my video get uploaded to your servers?

    No. Finding and cutting clips runs entirely in your browser, so your full video stays on your device. The only time anything is uploaded is when you tap Post now or Schedule on a clip: that one clip is sent through our posting partner, Upload-Post, to the accounts you connected, and nothing else.

    How do I connect my social media accounts?

    Sign in, then in Studio step 3 tap Connect accounts under Auto-post and sign in to each platform. Set each account up first with the 5-minute guides in Platforms. Snapchat doesn’t allow auto-posting yet, so you post those clips from your phone.

    How do I cancel?

    Any time, from the billing portal linked in your ClipNow billing emails, or just send us a message and we'll do it for you. No calls, no "are you sure" mazes. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. See our Refund Policy.

    Where are your privacy policy and terms?

    Read our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Refunds & Cancellation Policy. The short version: your videos never leave your device, payments are handled by Stripe, and we don't sell your information.
