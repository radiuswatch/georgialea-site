Help & Support

Questions about ClipNow, your plan or a charge? We're here to help.

Email us: helpdesk@clipnow.vip
We usually reply within two business days. Write from the email on your ClipNow account so we can find it quickly.

Billing and your plan

  • Cancel anytime: open the billing portal from any ClipNow billing email, or email us and we'll cancel for you. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Refunds: see our Refunds & Cancellation Policy. Email us with the date of the charge.
  • Update your card: use the billing portal link in your ClipNow emails, or email us and we'll send you a secure link.
  • Charged by mistake? Email us and we'll fix it. Duplicate or mistaken charges are refunded in full.

Signing in

ClipNow has no password. Enter the email you used at checkout and we'll email you a 6-digit sign-in code from helpdesk@clipnow.vip. If it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your spam or promotions folder, or request a new code.

Using ClipNow

On clipnow.vip, the Ask ClipNow chat (bottom right of the home page) answers questions about plans, posting, campaigns and getting paid, and the help videos walk you through making clips, scheduling posts and creating campaigns.
